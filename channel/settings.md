## What I learned from @thesticktales3000 (checked 2026-11-16)

Facts seen on their page: Shorts only, 23 videos, ~1.65k subscribers. Views by title (sample of 23, so treat as a hint, not a rule):

| Title pattern | Example | Views |
|---|---|---|

| Odd rule, money, power | "Why World Leaders Can't Keep Their Gifts" | 86k |
| "What would you do?" dilemma | "Found ₹1 Lakh on the Road… Keep It or Jail?" | 21k |
| Far-away history or product stories | "The Town That Paid Silence as Tax" | 1.6k |

What they do well (copy the *pattern*):
1. **English, one clear promise, one big hook line** in the title and on screen (a question or a shock).
2. **Bright flat banner and avatar** with one friendly mascot and a few bold words, readable at 98 px.
3. **Topics at the India × world crossover** and everyday "why" questions travel furthest; remote trivia stays small.
4. A title with **one emoji at the end** and no more than ~60 characters.

What not to copy:
- Their About text is a fair-use legal boilerplate; it sells nothing to a viewer. Ours tells people what they get.
- Their banner joke, mascot, scripts and topic list. Our cast is Sticky, Pinku, Gudiya and Dadi.
- Do **not** lean on trending creators or celebrities in titles to borrow views; that is spammy and risks a strike.

## Our three language lanes (one channel, three promises)

| Lane | Language | Series | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **English (global)** | English only | Stick Tales: true stories and "why" | UK, US, Canada, Gulf, Africa, SE Asia, Indian English viewers. The biggest audience |
| **हिन्दी** | Hindi (Devanagari) | Facts (Ajeeb Sach), Kahani | North India, Hindi speakers abroad |
| **Hinglish** | Hindi in English letters | Kahani, Cartoon (Pinku aur Dadi) | Young Indian viewers, the diaspora |

Rules that keep this from confusing viewers or YouTube:
- **One language per video.** Never mix English and Hindi narration in one Short. The Short's *own* language is set in upload settings.
- One playlist per series (below). The channel page stays a clean shelf, not a mixed pile.
- Every video still carries **English text** (title or first description line) and an **English subtitle track**, so a global viewer can follow a Hindi story.
- Suggested weekly mix once you can keep up: 3 English, 2 Hindi/Hinglish Facts or Kahani, 1 Cartoon. Fixed daily slot (calendar.md).

## Per-video recipe for global reach (every Short)

1. **Hook** in the first 2 s, spoken and on screen. A question or a one-line shock.
2. **Title (English)**, 40–60 characters, hook first, optional one emoji at the end.
   Hindi/Hinglish videos: story-language title + English line at the top of the description.
3. **Two subtitle tracks** (`captions-en.srt` and `captions-<lang>.srt`) with the same timings.
4. **Translated title + description** (Studio → video → Subtitles → three dots → *Add title and description*) for Hindi plus 2–3 more languages where a topic fits (e.g. Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Arabic). Do this by hand or with a translator you trust; read it before you save.
5. **Topic test:** does it work for someone who has never been to India? Add an India angle only where it is the real story (Diwali, UPI, the Taj, spices); that crossover is what travelled furthest on the channel we studied.
6. **Pinned comment:** one question for the audience, or "Next: ...".
7. **Post time:** English Shorts at 21:30 IST (about 5 pm UK, noon US East); Hindi/Hinglish Shorts at 19:00 IST. Keep each slot 30 days before changing.
8. Answer comments in the first hour; ask viewers what to cover next and **actually use** the good ones.

## Verify the account (phone verification, 5 minutes, you do it)
Status on 2026-11-16: phone verification DONE. Standard and Intermediate features Enabled; Advanced shown as "Eligible" (needs an ID or video check, only when needed).
Verifying unlocks Intermediate features: custom thumbnails, videos over 15 minutes, live streaming, links in descriptions and the About page that people can click.
1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account that owns the channel; switch to the *Doodle Story Tales* channel (profile picture, then Switch account).
2. Go to https://www.youtube.com/verify (or Studio, then Settings, then Channel, then Feature eligibility, then 2. Intermediate features, then *Verify phone number*).
3. Choose your country (India) and how to get the code: text message or voice call.
4. Enter your own mobile number and press *Get code*.
5. Type the 6-digit code you receive, then *Submit*. Never share the code with anyone.
6. Back in Feature eligibility, 2. Intermediate features should now say *Enabled*.
7. For 3. Advanced features, open the section and follow *Verify with ID or video*. YouTube asks for extra checks; do this only when you need very long live streams or the Partner Programme.
Also do now: two-step verification on the Google account (myaccount.google.com, then Security), and add a recovery email.
