2. Raat gehri thi, aur Pinku ko raasta dikhai nahi de raha tha.

4. Dadi boli: andhere mein ek chhota diya bhi sabka raasta dikha deta hai.
5. Kal batati hoon, Pinku ne agla diya kahan rakha.

## 2. 11 din bina soye (Facts, Hindi)
1. क्या कोई इंसान 11 दिन तक बिना सोए रह सकता है?
2. 1964 में सैन डिएगो के रैंडी गार्डनर 264 घंटे जागते रहे।
3. स्टैनफ़ोर्ड के नींद-वैज्ञानिक डॉ. विलियम डिमेंट उन पर नज़र रखते रहे।
4. दो हफ़्ते बाद रिकॉर्ड टूटा, और गिनीज़ ने इसे रोक दिया, क्योंकि यह ख़तरनाक है।

Sources: NPR, "Lessons from sleeplessness: the 60th anniversary of Randy Gardner's world record" (2024-01-28),
https://www.npr.org/2024/01/28/1227217274/sleep-deprivation-record ·
Guinness World Records, "What's the limit to how long a human can stay awake?" (2023),
https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/1/whats-the-limit-to-how-long-a-human-can-stay-awake-733188
Checked: 11 days = 264 hours, January 1964, William Dement (Stanford) helped monitor, record broken about two weeks later, Guinness stopped tracking sleep deprivation because it is harmful.
Left out on purpose: his age, and the year Guinness stopped (sources disagree).

## 3. Birbal ki khichdi (Kahani, Hinglish, folk tale; say "jaise dadi sunati thi" in the caption)
1. Kya door rakhi aag par khichdi pak sakti hai?
2. Sardi ki raat thi. Badshah Akbar ne kaha: jo ek raat thande paani mein khada rahega, use inaam milega.
3. Ek gareeb aadmi raat bhar paani mein khada raha, aur door jalte ek diye ko dekhta raha.
4. Subah Akbar ne kaha: us diye ki garmi ne tumhe bachaya, isliye inaam nahi milega.
5. Aadmi Birbal ke paas gaya. Birbal ne ek handi aag se kaafi upar tangi, aur neeche chhoti si aag jalayi.
6. Der tak khichdi nahi pakki. Akbar hanse: itni door aag se khichdi kaise pakegi?
7. Birbal bole: jahanpanah, toh door ke diye se garmi kaise mili?
8. Akbar samajh gaye, aur gareeb ko inaam diya. Kal sunaiye: Birbal ki ek aur kahani.

Sources (retellings; details differ, so it is labelled a folk tale): USC Folklore, "Akbar and Birbal: Kichri Story", https://folklore.usc.edu/?p=25145 ·
Vedantu, "Akbar and Birbal" stories, https://www.vedantu.com/stories/akbar-and-birbal
No deities. Akbar is shown with dignity, no mockery.

## 4. Why does the moon follow us? (Cartoon, English only, Pinku and Dadi)
1. Why does the moon follow us when we walk?
2. Pinku and Dadi were walking home one night, and the moon walked with them.
3. Dadi pointed at the trees. They rushed past, one after another.
4. But the moon is about 384,000 kilometres away, so it hardly seems to move.
5. That is why it looks like it is walking with you. Tomorrow: why do stars twinkle?

Sources: Caltech/IPAC Cool Cosmos, "Why does the Moon follow me when I move?", https://coolcosmos.ipac.caltech.edu/ask/177-Why-does-the-Moon-follow-me-when-I-move- ·
EarthSky, "Moon and stars distance and appearance", https://earthsky.org/astronomy-essentials/moon-and-stars-distance-appearance/
Checked: motion parallax; the Moon is about 384,000 km (about 239,000 miles) away.

## 5. The dancing plague of 1518 (Facts, English only)
1. Why did hundreds of people dance until they collapsed?
2. In July 1518, a woman began dancing in a street in Strasbourg, and could not stop.
3. Within a month, hundreds had joined her, and some collapsed from exhaustion.
4. Nobody knows for sure why it happened, and historians still argue about it.

Sources: National Geographic, "The dancing plague of 1518, Strasbourg" , https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/dancing-plague-of-1518-strasbourg-choreomania ·
History Revealed, "The medieval dancing plague", https://historyrevealed.com/period/medieval/the-medieval-dance-of-death
Checked: July 1518, Strasbourg, Frau Troffea began dancing, spread to hundreds over about a month, some dancers collapsed or died, cause still debated (mass psychogenic illness is the leading modern view, not proven).
