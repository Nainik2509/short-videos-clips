## What I changed from your brief, and why
- **"Ticket collector in Ranchi" is wrong.** Wikipedia says he worked as a Travelling Ticket Examiner at Kharagpur (South Eastern Railway), 2001-2003.
- **Scope of the news.** Reports say he retired from IPL *playing* and becomes CSK mentor for 2027. One outlet (Onmanorama) says "all formats", but he already retired internationally on 15 August 2020, so the script says only "retired from the IPL".
- **IPL totals and age are not scripted.** Outlets disagree (278 matches / 5,439 runs overall versus 272 / 5,314 for CSK; age 42 or 45).
- **No downloaded clips.** Match footage belongs to the BCCI, IPL and broadcasters; the video is original 3D animation (see the footage note in my last message). Clips you own or have permission for can go in `raw-footage/`.
