How did a goalkeeper from Ranchi become the man India trusts with the last over? Stay till the end.
Ranchi, 1981. Young Dhoni stood in goal. Hitting sixes was not even on the list.
Then a train ticket examiner's job at Kharagpur. Platform by day, dream on the side.
2007. A new captain lifted the very first T20 World Cup.
But the moment India still replays was four years away.
April 2011, Mumbai. World Cup final. Ninety-one not out, and he finished it with a six. A billion people exhaled.
World champions again, after twenty-eight years.
In 2013, the Champions Trophy made him the only captain to win all three major ICC white-ball trophies.
Then came the yellow army. Five IPL titles with Chennai. They called him Captain Cool.
Today, after eighteen years, he retired from the IPL.
But hold on. He is not leaving. Chennai named him team mentor for 2027.
So how did he do it? By staying calm when everyone else could not. Some players finish matches. He finished doubts.
