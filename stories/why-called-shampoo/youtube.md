Why is it called shampoo?
Because it is a Hindi word. It comes from "champo", which means to press or massage.
In India, champo was a relaxing head massage with oil.
In the 1700s, the word reached English, and it still meant massage.
In 1814, an Indian man named Sake Dean Mahomed opened a bathhouse in Brighton, England, offering shampooing.
It was so popular that two British kings made him their Shampooing Surgeon.
Only around the 1860s did shampoo start to mean washing your hair.
