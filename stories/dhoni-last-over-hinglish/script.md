## Script (same 13 scenes as the English version; the facts and sources are identical)
1. **hook** Ranchi ka ek goalkeeper, last over ka sabse bharosemand khiladi kaise ban gaya? Video ke end tak ruko.
2. **ranchi** Ranchi, 1981. Chhote Dhoni goal mein khade rehte the. Chhakke maarna to list mein tha hi nahi.
3. **train** Phir Kharagpur station par train ticket examiner ki naukri. Din mein platform, sapna saath mein.
4. **t20** 2007. Ek naye captain ne pehla T20 World Cup utha liya.
5. **loop** Lekin jis pal ko India aaj bhi baar baar dekhta hai, woh chaar saal door tha.
6. **final** April 2011, Mumbai. World Cup final. 91 not out, aur chhakke se match khatam. Karodon dilon ne ek saath saans li.
7. **champions** Atthaaees saal baad, India phir world champion.
8. **three** 2013 mein Champions Trophy ne unhe banaya aisa akela captain, jisne teeno bade ICC white-ball trophy jeete.
9. **csk** Phir aayi yellow army. Chennai ke saath paanch IPL titles. Log unhe Captain Cool kehte the.
10. **retire** Aaj, atharah saal baad, unhone IPL se retirement le li.
11. **mentor** Lekin ruko. Woh ja nahi rahe. Chennai ne unhe 2027 ke liye team mentor banaya hai.
12. **answer** To unhone yeh kaise kiya? Shaant rehkar, jab baaki sab ghabra jaate the. Kuch khiladi match khatam karte hain. Unhone shak khatam kiye.
13. **next** Agla part: 2011 final, ball by ball. Follow karo, taaki miss na ho.
