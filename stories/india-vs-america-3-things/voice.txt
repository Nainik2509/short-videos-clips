Three things that are normal in India, but hard to find in America. Stick around for number three.
One: UPI. From luxury stores to chai stalls, you just scan and pay. Chai, scan, done.
America still leans on cards, and banks collected about 66 billion dollars in swipe fees last year. That is a lot of swiping.
Two: mobile data. Indian plans with unlimited 5G start at a few hundred rupees a month. Scroll more, pay less.
In America, one line on a big carrier starts around 45 dollars.
Now, number three: medicine. Many Indian pharmacies sell common medicines with no doctor visit.
In America, many need a prescription, and an uninsured visit averages about 170 dollars.
One catch: antibiotics need a prescription in India too. Health first, always.
