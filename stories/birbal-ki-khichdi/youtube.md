Transcript (English):
Can khichdi cook on a fire kept far away?
It was a winter night. Emperor Akbar said: whoever stands in cold water for one night will get a reward.
A poor man stood in the water all night, looking at a lamp burning far away.
In the morning Akbar said: the warmth of that lamp saved you, so there is no reward.
The man went to Birbal. Birbal hung a pot high above a fire, and lit a tiny flame below.
The khichdi did not cook. Akbar laughed: how can khichdi cook on a fire so far away?
Birbal said: Your Majesty, then how did a faraway lamp give warmth?
Akbar understood, and gave the poor man his reward. Tomorrow: another Birbal story.

Transcript:
Kya door rakhi aag par khichdi pak sakti hai?
Sardi ki raat thi. Badshah Akbar ne kaha: jo ek raat thande paani mein khada rahega, use inaam milega.
Ek gareeb aadmi raat bhar paani mein khada raha, aur door jalte ek diye ko dekhta raha.
Subah Akbar ne kaha: us diye ki garmi ne tumhe bachaya, isliye inaam nahi milega.
Aadmi Birbal ke paas gaya. Birbal ne ek handi aag se kaafi upar tangi, aur neeche chhoti si aag jalayi.
Der tak khichdi nahi pakki. Akbar hanse: itni door aag se khichdi kaise pakegi?
Birbal bole: jahanpanah, toh door ke diye se garmi kaise mili?
Akbar samajh gaye, aur gareeb ko inaam diya. Kal sunaiye: Birbal ki ek aur kahani.

Note: this is a folk tale, retold the way Dadi told it. Versions differ.
