## Script (one scene per line; also in `voice.txt`)
1. Kya door rakhi aag par khichdi pak sakti hai?
2. Sardi ki raat thi. Badshah Akbar ne kaha: jo ek raat thande paani mein khada rahega, use inaam milega.
3. Ek gareeb aadmi raat bhar paani mein khada raha, aur door jalte ek diye ko dekhta raha.
4. Subah Akbar ne kaha: us diye ki garmi ne tumhe bachaya, isliye inaam nahi milega.
5. Aadmi Birbal ke paas gaya. Birbal ne ek handi aag se kaafi upar tangi, aur neeche chhoti si aag jalayi.
6. Der tak khichdi nahi pakki. Akbar hanse: itni door aag se khichdi kaise pakegi?
7. Birbal bole: jahanpanah, toh door ke diye se garmi kaise mili?
8. Akbar samajh gaye, aur gareeb ko inaam diya. Kal sunaiye: Birbal ki ek aur kahani.
