Why does your airplane window have a tiny hole?
Look closely. Each window has three layers, and the middle one has a hole.
Up high, the cabin is pressurised, so the air inside pushes hard on the glass.
The hole lets that pressure reach the outer pane, so the outer pane carries the load.
That way, if a pane ever fails, it is the outer one, not the one next to you.
The hole also lets moisture escape, so your window stays clear.
