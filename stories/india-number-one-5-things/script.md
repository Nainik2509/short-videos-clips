## What I changed from your text, and why (all for accuracy)
- **Milk: "over 22%" → "nearly a quarter".** Current figures put India at about 24–25% of world milk. "More than the EU combined" holds (India ~230–240 million tonnes, EU ~150–160).
- **Post offices: "over 150,000" → "about 165,000".** India Post's own count is 164,999 (31 March 2025).
- **Spices: "producing 70% of global spices" removed.** The 70% figure is old and not supported today; the Spices Board / FAO-based figures range from about 25% to 45%. What every source agrees on is that India is the largest producer and exporter, so the line says that, with no percentage.
- **Films: "Bollywood releases more than 1,500 films" → "India makes more than 1,500 films a year in more than 20 languages".** Bollywood is only the Hindi industry (a few hundred films a year); the 1,500+ figure is all Indian cinema. RRR is a Telugu film, so "Bollywood's famous films" would also be wrong for it. "Twice as many as Hollywood" is dropped because I found no reliable yearly Hollywood count to compare with.
- **Antilia: "worth $2 billion" → "valued at around two billion dollars", "often called".** The value is an estimate (reports range from $1 billion to over $4 billion) and "most expensive" is a widely repeated label, not a record. The staff of 600 is reported, not confirmed, so the line says "reported". No person is shown.
- **"Here's what they do differently" removed.** The video lists rankings, it does not explain methods, so the promise would not be kept.
- **Ending added** (channel rule: answer, then one line that opens the next one).
