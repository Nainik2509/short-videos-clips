Did you know India ranks number one in the world in these five things?
Number one. India produces nearly a quarter of the world's milk.
That's more than the entire European Union combined.
Number two. With about 165,000 post offices, India has the largest postal network on Earth.
One of them even floats, on Dal Lake in Kashmir.
Number three. India is the spice capital of the world, the biggest grower and exporter of spices.
No wonder Indian food is so delicious.
Number four. India makes more films than any other country, over 1,500 a year, in more than 20 languages.
Its biggest hits include Dangal, RRR and Jawan.
And number five. India is home to Antilia, often called the world's most expensive private home.
Valued at around two billion dollars, this Mumbai tower has 27 floors, 3 helipads and a reported staff of 600.
